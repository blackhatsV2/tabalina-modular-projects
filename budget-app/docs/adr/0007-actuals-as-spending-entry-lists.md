# `actuals` holds a list of spending entries per day, not one lump amount

The original data model (CLAUDE.md step 1) gave each date in `actuals` a single `{amount, note?}` — one logged figure per day. In practice people don't log a day's spending as one number: they log fare, lunch, and water separately, as each happens. Forcing that into one field would mean either overwriting the previous entry on a second log of the same day, or inventing an ad hoc "add to existing amount" convention that loses the individual notes. Decided instead that `actuals` holds a list per date — `{[dateISO]: {id, amount, note?, category?}[]}` — where each entry is independently editable and deletable by its own `id`, and a day's actual total is always the sum of its entries, computed on read and never cached as a separate stored field (consistent with the project's general stance against redundant derived storage).

This doesn't touch `simulate()` or the parity test: the engine never reads `actuals` (it's a planned-vs-actual comparison, not simulation input), and the Jayrold fixture's `actuals` is already `{}`. The change is confined to `src/model/schema.ts` and `src/model/parse.ts`. `category` is a free-text optional field, same convention as `modules.bills` items' `category` — not an enum, and not required to match any bill's category.
